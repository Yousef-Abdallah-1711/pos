## Method

All 84 capability/discovery records from the inventory are included. The “actual capability” column describes source behavior, not live readiness. Every operational explanation is absent under the ten-module definition in the current-state report. The lone public-localization record is explicit coverage. The UI image contains incidental names; those are treated separately from explanatory marketing copy. `Features.tsx`, `translations.ts`, `FAQ.tsx` and the live DOM provide the landing evidence for all rows.

**Marketing gap** means missing explanation for an established, eventually releasable behavior. **Product/verification gap** means missing behavior, known inconsistency or missing workflow acceptance; it must not be solved by adding promises. In the matrix, partial/not-found rows are product/verification gaps first; implemented-unverified rows are verification plus potential content gaps.
