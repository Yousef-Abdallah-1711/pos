# Visual baseline plan

An initial authenticated synthetic baseline is available in [baseline/README.md](baseline/README.md): 15 captures cover the owner dashboard in EN/AR and both themes, collapsed desktop and the Arabic mobile drawer, empty POS and KDS, one POS cart, orders, products, inventory, employees, reports and settings. These are development-mode captures from a synthetic restaurant; their exact image source commit was not independently recorded. They do not cover restricted roles, most populated workflows or the full route/theme/device matrix. Continue with appearance, storefront, and other routes in applicable EN/AR × light/dark variants. Record route, source commit, timestamp, viewport, device scale, data provenance and redactions for each future image. Never copy cookies, credentials or real operational records into evidence; do not mistake browser emulation for physical-device evidence.
