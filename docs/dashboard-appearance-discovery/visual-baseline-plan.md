# Visual baseline plan

An initial authenticated synthetic baseline is available in [baseline/README.md](baseline/README.md): eight captures cover the owner dashboard in EN/AR and both themes, collapsed desktop and the Arabic mobile drawer, plus empty POS and KDS screens. These are development-mode captures with an empty synthetic restaurant; their exact image source commit was not independently recorded. They do not cover restricted roles, populated workflows or the full route/theme/device matrix. Continue with orders, products, inventory, staff, reports, appearance and storefront in applicable EN/AR × light/dark variants. Record route, source commit, timestamp, viewport, device scale, data provenance and redactions for each future image. Never copy cookies, credentials or real operational records into evidence; do not mistake browser emulation for physical-device evidence.
