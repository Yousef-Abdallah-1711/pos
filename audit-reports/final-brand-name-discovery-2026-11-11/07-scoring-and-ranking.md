## Shortlist decision

Only Ularo, Cendro and Flarivo advance as **INCONCLUSIVE research leads**, not passes. The remaining ranked names have stronger identified commercial conflicts and are removed from owner consideration. The full 32-name concept set and first-pass screen are in `02-name-generation-30-candidates.md` and `03-commercial-conflict-screen.md`.

**Deep top-10 verification was not completed.** The search signals for ranks 5–10 were sufficient to remove them from the shortlist, but official trademark, domain and social checks were not conducted for those names. For the three remaining leads, no candidate has the evidence required for a preliminary pass. A scoring model cannot substitute for customer testing or legal review.
