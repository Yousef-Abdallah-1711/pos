### Domain strategy

Do not buy or announce a domain before the owner selects a legally cleared name. For Egypt-first operations, ask an accredited registrar to verify both the intended `.com.eg` eligibility/availability and the corresponding international domain. If the `.com` is already registered, do not assume an alternative TLD provides a clean identity: exact-name software use and practical customer confusion matter more than finding an unoccupied suffix. No purchase, reservation, registrant identity inference or registrar checkout was performed.
