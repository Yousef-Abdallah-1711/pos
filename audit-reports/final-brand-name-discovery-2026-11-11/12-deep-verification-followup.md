## Deep commercial review of the three leads

### Ularo — أولارو — oo-LAH-roh — INCONCLUSIVE

- **Exact / spelling / phonetic search:** focused exact searches for `Ularo`, restaurant/POS/software terms, Egypt/MENA terms, and Arabic `أولارو` did not surface a current restaurant-management SaaS product. Public results did surface a Polish `ULARO SERVICE` company and an older Ularo Constructions Android app. The app was removed from Google Play in 2023 according to the app-directory record; it is not evidence of current activity.
- **Commercial context:** the Polish entity is reported in secondary company indexes as active and in office-administration/support activity, not restaurant software. It remains a same-name commercial entity and needs primary registry confirmation. A brochure search result containing “Ularo” was a likely false positive: the document is for **Uluro**, and its text says “Ularo” among trademarks; do not count it as a verified Ularo product without checking the original document context.
- **Restaurant technology / app stores / company directories:** no relevant restaurant-tech competitor was established in the focused search. One old, unrelated construction-services app surfaced. Search engines and public app indexes are incomplete.
- **Arabic / phonetics:** `أولارو` is a workable reading for “oo-LAH-roh,” but `U` can be read differently by English speakers and Arabic users may render the opening vowel inconsistently. Test with Egyptian Arabic and English speakers.
- **Risk:** exact-name business use and registered `.com`; no official mark or social clearance. The present evidence is insufficient to call the name clear or recommend it.

### Cendro — سيندرو — SEN-droh — CONFLICT FLAGGED

- **Exact / spelling / phonetic search:** exact searches surfaced the active Cendro Archive and Cendro Cloud products at `cendro.net`. The owner describes the products as SaaS for secure cloud archiving, searchable document storage and governance. Other uses include a Cendro dental-imaging app, a Cendro Technologies web-analytics company, a Cendro Solutions software/AI consultancy, and unrelated commercial uses.
- **Restaurant technology:** no restaurant POS product was found in the focused queries. The principal conflict is exact-name software/SaaS and adjacent business-management software, not a demonstrated restaurant competitor.
- **Official trademark result:** IP Australia's [official result for CENDRO no. 2702587](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/view/2702587?q=CENDRO) shows a word mark filed 2026-10-05, “Published: Awaiting examination” / “Application Being Considered,” owner Southwest Onsite Data Backup Management Pty. Ltd., classes 9, 35 and 42, including software/SaaS for document and data storage, archiving and governance. The register shows no opposition information and expected report issue 2027-01-04. This is an Australian pending application, not a registration or a legal opinion about rights in Egypt/MENA.
- **Arabic / phonetics:** `سيندرو` approximates “SEN-droh,” but Arabic vowel length and the /d/ cluster should be tested. It can also be heard as a transliteration rather than a distinctive coined word.
- **Risk:** the same wordmark is already used for a current software platform, plus secondary reports of a pending Australian mark in overlapping software classes. This makes the exact name unsuitable to advance absent a specialist clearance opinion; it is flagged even without proving legal conflict in Egypt/MENA.
