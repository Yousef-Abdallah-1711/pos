## Recommendation versus previous names

**Recommend NONE.** Ularo has the lightest observed restaurant-software signal among the three, but the exact-name entity, registered `.com`, incomplete official register checks and missing social checks leave too much uncertainty to call it strongest with reasonable confidence. Cendro has the strongest direct same-name software conflict. Flarivo has unrelated exact commercial use and incomplete clearance.

| Existing name | Current evidence and relative assessment |
|---|---|
| **SERVIO** | Prior naming work rejected it for commercial overlap; no new evidence here reverses that finding. Do not treat it as cleared. |
| **TAVRO** | Prior gate flagged close restaurant POS/service competitors (Tavero Tech and Tarro) and multiple active software identities. Higher apparent category-confusion risk than Ularo/Flarivo. Not cleared. |
| **RAVINO** | Targeted search found Ravino AI for finance/insurance, a Ravino retailer of restaurant POS terminals, and a secondary index reporting EU application raVino no. 019277713 covering SaaS/software, reportedly opposed. | **Sabviro** | Earlier TAVRO fallback screen found no exact commercial result and no `.com` RDAP object, but official marks/socials and pronunciation were not checked. It is a research lead, not cleared or stronger on verified evidence alone. |
| **Ularo** | Better bilingual ease and lower observed restaurant-software signal, offset by exact-name company use, registered `.com`, incomplete trademark/social work. Inconclusive. |
| **Cendro** | Clear pronunciation and plausible concept, but active exact-name data-archiving SaaS and an official pending Australian software-class trademark application make it the weakest on commercial-confusion risk. Conflict flagged. |
| **Flarivo** | Energetic but less effortless Arabic phonetics, exact-name media company, registered `.com`, incomplete marks/socials. Inconclusive. |

No quantitative winner is justified. The prior score table is a research-priority tool and should not be read as a probability of legal clearance. Candidate rankings should be recalculated only after primary trademark searches and audience testing.
