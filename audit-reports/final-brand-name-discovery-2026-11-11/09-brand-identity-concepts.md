# Brand identity concepts — exploratory only

These are three directions, not finished brand systems.

| Candidate | English wordmark | Arabic wordmark | Symbol | Personality / color direction | Tagline concept | Product architecture |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ularo | Humanist geometric lowercase; distinctive open “u” | Custom Arabic lettering with balanced open counters; do not transliterate mechanically | Open doorway/serving pass with three connected nodes | Calm, hospitable, capable; preserve purple premium direction and add warm copper accent | “Every shift, in sync.” / «كل وردية… على إيقاع واحد» | Ularo POS, Ularo Kitchen, Ularo Stock, Ularo People, Ularo Sites, Ularo Insights |
| Cendro | Compact geometric sans; central “d” counter as device | Clear سـ / نـ connection; test letter spacing at small sizes | Central node and radial service paths | Precise, coordinated; purple plus cool slate and restrained citrus | “One clear view of service.” / «صورة أوضح لكل التشغيل» | Cendro POS, Cendro Kitchen, Cendro Inventory, Cendro Team, Cendro Online, Cendro Analytics |
| Flarivo | Rounded but restrained; emphasize distinctive “v” | Fl- cluster requires Arabic-speaker test; use a smooth phonetic form only after validation | Spark resolving into a service line | Warm, energetic; purple plus saffron accent | “Make every service flow.” / «خلّي الخدمة تمشي بسلاسة» | Flarivo POS, Flarivo Kitchen, Flarivo Stock, Flarivo Team, Flarivo Online, Flarivo Insights |

No logos, production assets, live design changes or public copy were created. Validate marks with Arabic and English speakers and avoid competitor imitation.
