# SERVIO Name Gate — Preliminary Conflict Screen

**Screened:** 2026-11-11  
**Decision:** **CONFLICT FLAGGED — do not publish SERVIO as the product brand or commission/register its logo pending professional clearance and owner direction.**  
**Scope:** Preliminary public-web screen only. This is not legal advice, a complete trademark search, or a clearance opinion.

## Material market conflicts

Search results and the businesses' own public pages show multiple restaurant software businesses already using **Servio/SERVIO** for overlapping services:

| Existing use | Public evidence | Overlap |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Servio Food | [serviofood.com](https://serviofood.com/) offers restaurant POS and SaaS for billing, orders, kitchen operations, deliveries, inventory, and multi-branch control. | Direct restaurant POS and operations overlap. |

| SERVIO | [LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/servio-tech) identifies a Makkah-based Saudi restaurant/café ordering and payment platform connected to cashier and kitchen operations. | Exact name and directly relevant MENA market. |

The number of exact-name businesses in the same category—including one in Saudi Arabia—makes this a material commercial and confusion risk. It is enough to fail the brief's preliminary naming gate; it does **not** establish who owns enforceable rights in Egypt or any particular market.

## Trademark screening status

- **Egypt:** Unverified. The official [ITDA Trade Names Gazette](https://www.itda.gov.eg/en/jurnal-en.html) says searches can be made by trade mark after selecting an issue/month and office. The [ITDA digital services portal](https://itda.gov.eg/en/registry-en.html) lists trade-name and non-confusion inquiries. No official, exhaustive Egypt trademark clearance search or certified non-confusion result was obtained in this pass.
- **EU:** A secondary trademark-data page reports application **019357935** for “Servio” covering SaaS/PaaS restaurant online-ordering systems, marked opposed in August 2026. Treat this only as a lead to verify directly in [EUIPO/TMview](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/trade-marks/before-applying/availability); this report does not rely on the secondary status as an official finding.
- **Other expansion markets:** Unverified. USPTO, WIPO, and national registers require a goods/services- and jurisdiction-specific search. A basic web search cannot clear the mark.

## Domain and social checks

- The root domain [servio.com](https://servio.com/) is already registered and redirects to an unrelated commercial site; it is not available for immediate use based on this check.
- Restaurant-software businesses actively use `servio.solutions`, `serviofood.com`, `servio.uk`, `servio.rest`, `serviotables.com`, and `useservio.io`.
- The exact SERVIO name is already used by restaurant software organizations on LinkedIn, including the Saudi page above. Other social handle availability and ownership were not verified. No handles should be reserved or represented as official.
- `servio.com.eg` and other country domains were not confirmed through an authoritative registrar/RDAP lookup. Do not infer that they are available.

## Safe next steps

1. Keep the existing public POOS identity unchanged while the SERVIO name gate is unresolved.
2. Do not publish a SERVIO rebrand, commit or push SERVIO marketing changes, buy domains, open social accounts, or file trademark applications.
3. Ask qualified trademark counsel to search Egypt and the intended expansion territories in the relevant software, POS, restaurant, and SaaS classes; include Arabic transliterations and similar marks. Obtain the owner's approval for any formal filing or outreach.
4. If the owner selects a different name, repeat the market and registry screen before designing and publishing its identity.
