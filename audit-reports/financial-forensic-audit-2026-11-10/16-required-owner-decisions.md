# Required owner decisions

1. Which jurisdictions and tax registrations are in scope? Is a 10% default ever valid, or must tax be configured by item/order?
2. Which accounting framework and statutory formats apply? Obtain qualified accountant advice.
3. Cash or accrual basis by report and restaurant? When is revenue recognized for dine-in, delivery and marketplaces?
4. Inventory costing method, recipes, waste/spoilage, stocktake and COGS timing?
5. Supported currencies, FX source, rounding mode, tips, discounts, service charges, delivery fees and marketplace commissions?
6. Refund/void/chargeback treatment: reversal journal, liability, fees, partial refund and settlement timing?
7. Fiscal year, closed periods, approvals, journal numbering, correction and retention policy?
8. Is general ledger the only authoritative financial record, or are operational aggregates authoritative in some reports?
9. Which roles may create, approve, post, void, export or close records? Is maker-checker separation required?
10. Which local authority or specialist approves VAT, invoice, audit export and retention rules?

These choices require business and accounting owners. Engineering should encode policy, not invent it.
