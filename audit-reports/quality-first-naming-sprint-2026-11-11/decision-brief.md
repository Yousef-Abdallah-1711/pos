# Quality-first restaurant SaaS naming sprint

**Research date:** 2026-11-11  
**Current public brand:** POOS  
**Decision:** No candidate is ready to recommend for adoption. No rebrand has started.

## Scope and method

Generated exactly 12 candidates and carried the strongest eight into public commercial search: Nawba, Siniya, Wasla, Mizan, Sanad, Mawrid, Riwaq, and Dawra. Search scope included exact English spellings, Arabic spellings/terms, restaurant/POS/software combinations, and available indexed company/product pages. Search-engine non-results are weak evidence and do not establish non-use.

The strongest five received domain-registration-object checks for `.com`, `.io`, `.app`, and `.co`. A registry object means the name is registered; no object does not guarantee availability or eligibility. `.com.eg` was not verified. Exact-name official trademark searches were previously run in IP Australia and USPTO for the top five. They were not comprehensive similarity or clearance searches. Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, WIPO, and EUIPO/TMview did not yield candidate-level results in this sprint.

## Official trademark research

Candidate-level exact English-word searches for **Nawba, Siniya, Wasla, Mizan, and Sanad** were run in the official USPTO and IP Australia search interfaces.

- **IP Australia:** exact Nawba, Siniya, and Wasla queries returned zero records; Mizan returned four records including MIZAN marks in class 9; Sanad returned five records. Exact-name searches do not evaluate similar spellings, sounds, Arabic variants, goods/services overlap, or other countries. [Official IP Australia search](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search)
- **USPTO:** exact Nawba and Wasla queries returned no results; Siniya returned a live registered class 2 mark for colorants; Mizan returned 15 results, including an exact pending MIZAN SaaS mark in class 42; Sanad returned five results, with no direct exact restaurant-software mark identified in the displayed results. This is not a full search report or legal opinion. [Official USPTO search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/)
- **Egypt / ITDA:** no candidate-level result. The portal lookup encountered a TLS/access failure in this research run; the commercial-name inquiry service was not purchased.
- **Saudi Arabia / SAIP:** official search service identified, but no candidate-level query/result captured.
- **UAE / Ministry of Economy:** official inquiry requires paid service; no query submitted.
- **WIPO Global Brand Database / Madrid:** no candidate-level result captured; the search interface presented a CAPTCHA/anti-bot barrier. WIPO itself advises searching national/regional offices as well. [WIPO search guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/how_to/search/index)
- **EUIPO / TMview:** no candidate-level result captured; the dynamic search interface returned no readable results to this tool. [EUIPO availability guidance](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/trade-marks/before-applying/availability)

This research does **not** establish legal clearance. Even official search portals warn that identical-name results are not exhaustive and similar marks must be considered; qualified trademark counsel should conduct a similarity and goods/services analysis in the target markets.

## Decision-ready view

No candidate qualifies as a **PRELIMINARY PASS**. The only possible watchlist leads are:

| Candidate | Status | Reason to retain for possible further research | Main unresolved risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Siniya / صينية** | **INCONCLUSIVE** | No direct restaurant SaaS conflict surfaced in this search set; name is short and visually suggestive. | Common tray word, inconsistent transliteration, registered `.com`, and incomplete trademark/social checks. |
| **Nawba / نوبة** | **INCONCLUSIVE** | Short, pronounceable in Arabic, and the shift metaphor fits restaurant operations. | Adjacent shift-calendar app and registered `.app`; `.com` acquisition cost/ownership unknown; marks/socials incomplete. |
| **Riwaq / رواق** | **INCONCLUSIVE** | Memorable Arabic word with a place/operations metaphor. | Hospitality-adjacent marketplace and hotel PMS; English pronunciation/spelling uncertainty; domains and marks not checked in this sprint. |

Other screened names are **CONFLICT FLAGGED** for direct restaurant-software or same-name SaaS uses: Wasla, Mizan, Sanad, Mawrid, and Dawra. Riwaq has adjacent hospitality identity uses but no restaurant POS product was established in these searches. These labels describe screening evidence, not a legal conclusion.

**Best brand recommendation: NONE.** Brand quality alone does not outweigh direct category collisions or missing key-market clearance. The fastest path to a decision is:

1. Owner selects whether to commission full clearance for **Siniya and Nawba only**; keep Riwaq as optional if the hospitality-adjacent uses are judged acceptable.
2. A trademark professional searches Arabic and Latin variants, phonetics, relevant classes, company/trade names, and use in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and UAE; then checks WIPO/Madrid and intended expansion markets.
3. An accredited registrar confirms `.com` and `.com.eg` eligibility/cost; directly check social handles after legal screening.
4. Conduct short pronunciation and recall tests with Egyptian Arabic, Gulf Arabic, and English speakers before selecting a finalist.
5. Owner approves a name only after those results. No public rebrand is authorized by this research.
