## Competing sources of truth (summary)
1. Revenue: orders (Sales, Profitability, KPI, order stats) vs GL 4100.
2. Cash: GL 1110/1111 vs `bank_accounts_ledger.current_balance` vs `chart_of_accounts.current_balance` cache vs KPI/Cash-flow formulas.
3. Inventory: `products.quantity_in_stock` × cost vs GL 1130 vs `inventory_movements`.
4. VAT: GL 2120/1150 vs `invoices_*.tax_amount` (report) vs `orders.tax_amount`.
5. Supplier balance: `suppliers.current_balance` vs GL 2101 vs `invoices_supplier.balance_due`.
