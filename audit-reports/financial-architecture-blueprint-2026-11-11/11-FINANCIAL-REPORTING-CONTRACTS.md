# 11 — Financial reporting contracts (PROPOSED)

Use posted journal lines at a declared consistent posting watermark. Exclude drafts; include reversals; disclose pending source events. Bind tenant, restaurant/consolidation scope, effective and recorded cutoff, currency, policy version and watermark. Backdated entries require versioned restatement/export marker.

- Trial Balance: account debits/credits, exact equality.
- General Ledger: posted lines and stable ordered balances.
- Balance Sheet: assets = liabilities + equity including current earnings/retained earnings; FIN-001 historical failure.
- P&L: revenue less contra-revenue, COGS and expenses; ties to earnings bridge.
- Cash Flow: approved operating/investing/financing classification; opening + movement = closing cash.
- AR/AP aging and statements: subledger equals GL control account.
- Inventory valuation: method-based quantity/cost equals inventory control.
- Tax/VAT: approved tax categories; never infer rates.
- Consolidation: mapped entity accounts, explicit eliminations.
- Exports: same filters, cutoff, precision and watermark as UI.
