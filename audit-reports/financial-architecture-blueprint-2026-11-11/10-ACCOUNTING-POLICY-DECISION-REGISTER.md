# 10 — Accounting policy decision register

All items remain OWNER or ACCOUNTANT DECISION REQUIRED.

| Decision | Options/trade-off | Provisional stance | Approval/dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jurisdiction/framework | statutory, IFRS-based, other | unset | accountant/legal; blocks final statements |
| Basis and revenue timing | cash/accrual, fulfillment/control | accrual proposal only | accountant/owner |
| Tax | jurisdiction, category, inclusive/exempt | no rate assumed | accountant/legal; FIN-005 |
| Inventory/COGS | FIFO, weighted average, other | unset | accountant/owner |
| Period and close | fiscal calendar, lock/reopen | unset | owner/accountant; FIN-001 |
| Currency/FX | single/multi, rate source/time | unset | owner/accountant |
| Rounding | line/invoice, mode, tolerance | no silent tolerance | accountant |
| Tips/deposits/gift cards | liability vs revenue timing | liability pending policy | accountant/legal |
| Refunds/chargebacks/marketplace | contra-revenue, tax, fee treatment | linked original allocation | accountant/payment owner |
| Roles, approvals, retention | thresholds, retention | least privilege proposal | owner/security/legal |

Record approver, date, rationale, policy version and impacted tasks. No legal or tax compliance claim.
