# TAVRO naming gate and fallback screen

**Screen date:** 2026-11-11  
**Scope:** Preliminary public-source and `.com` RDAP screening for a proposed restaurant operations SaaS brand. This is not legal advice or formal trademark clearance.

## Decision

**Preferred name:** TAVRO  
**Naming gate:** **CONFLICT FLAGGED**  
**Recommendation:** Do not adopt TAVRO. Keep the current public POOS brand until the owner approves a candidate after appropriate clearance. The evidence below is sufficient to flag risk; it does not establish infringement or legal unavailability.

The strongest low-signal candidate from this limited screen is **Sabviro** (provisional pronunciation: *sab-VEE-roh*, Arabic rendering for discussion: **سابفيرو**). It had no exact-match commercial result in the focused public web searches performed here, and Verisign `.com` RDAP returned no record for `sabviro.com` on the screen date. That is only a research lead, not a clearance or availability guarantee. Its pronunciation and spelling need owner/customer validation. Do not publish it without owner approval and formal trademark/domain checks.

## Trademark and official-register status

No comprehensive official-register search was completed for TAVRO or the alternatives. Egypt's [ITDA Trade Names Gazette](https://www.itda.gov.eg/en/jurnal-en.html) requires selecting an issue and office; ITDA lists a non-confusion inquiry through its [digital services portal](https://www.itda.gov.eg/en/registry-en.html). The EU's [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) includes Egypt data according to the [EUIPO network notice](https://www.tmdn.org/network/news/-/asset_publisher/Zf7xCoedavnJ/content/egypt-joins-tmview). [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) and [EUIPO availability guidance](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/trade-marks/before-applying/availability) are appropriate additional research tools. No verified search results from those interfaces are represented here.

Before selecting any name, obtain an attorney-led search in Egypt and target markets, including relevant Nice classes (at least software/SaaS and restaurant services as advised by counsel), exact and phonetic variants, Arabic transliterations, company/trade names and unregistered use. Trademark clearance is jurisdiction- and goods/services-specific.
