# Brand Typography

The landing page declares Alexandria and IBM Plex Sans Arabic with Arabic and Latin subsets, plus Manrope for Latin text. The social package follows that hierarchy.

| Role | Family | Weights used |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic wordmark and display headings | Alexandria | 700 |
| Arabic supporting copy | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | 400–600 |
| English wordmark and labels | Manrope | 700–800 |

Arabic copy uses right-to-left direction, right alignment, and the Arabic font subset. English wordmarks use Manrope with left-to-right text. Keep mixed-script lines separate where possible to avoid bidirectional reordering.

The relevant WOFF2 subsets are included under ../fonts/ with the upstream SIL Open Font License texts. SVG masters embed their font data so they render consistently and remain editable as text.
