# ORDERLY Brand Identity

## Approved name for this collateral

- English: **ORDERLY**
- Arabic: **أوردرلي**
- Approval: user-confirmed in this task on 13 November 2026.
- Scope: social launch assets only. This package does not rename or modify the application.

The live website and frontend/apps/landing/PRODUCT.md currently identify the software as **POOS**. The user explicitly chose ORDERLY for this social package. Coordinate any public account transition before posting these assets; do not mix the two names in one placement.

## Product and audience

The product is a restaurant management platform for independent restaurant owners and operators, plus teams managing multi-location restaurant groups. Its approved differentiator is one platform for front-of-house and back-of-house work.

The package avoids unverified feature lists, prices, trials, customer counts, testimonials, performance results, mobile apps, and tax or accounting claims. It uses no synthetic product screenshots.

## Positioning and voice

**Brand promise:** Give restaurant teams one place to keep front-of-house and back-of-house work connected.

**Personality:** Premium, modern, trustworthy, clean, distinctive, technology-focused, and business-oriented.

**Egyptian Arabic voice:** Clear and conversational, with familiar phrasing such as “قدّام الضيوف” and “لورا الكواليس.” Keep the message practical and calm. Use Arabic punctuation naturally and set Arabic copy right-to-left.

**Core campaign line:** “من قدّام الضيوف لورا الكواليس.”

## Logo rationale

Three directions were considered:

1. **Open O route (selected):** a continuous open loop forms the initial O in ORDERLY, with two terminal nodes suggesting a connected path through restaurant work. It remains legible at avatar and favicon sizes.
2. **Connected work cards:** a compact group of linked rounded cards. Clear at large sizes, but more likely to read as generic dashboard iconography.
3. **Order ticket index:** an abstract ticket edge with a route line. Restaurant-relevant, but more literal and less distinctive at small sizes.

The open O route was selected for recognition, small-size clarity, and a direct connection to the ORDERLY name without fork-and-spoon clip art. The artwork is original SVG. Wordmarks remain editable text and embed the selected font subsets in the SVG.

## Typography hierarchy

- English wordmark and English supporting labels: **Manrope**, bold.
- Arabic headings and Arabic wordmark: **Alexandria**, bold.
- Arabic supporting text: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**, regular or medium.
- Arabic text uses right-to-left direction and logical right alignment. Font binaries and their OFL licenses are in ../fonts/.

These are the families specified by the live product landing page and PRODUCT.md; this package does not introduce a new font family.

## Visual system

Use the violet and lavender palette from the product website. Keep most surfaces light and calm, with violet for brand marks and important actions. Use the ink color for readable text and the deep violet for dark placements. Use line-based workflow motifs and simple layered cards to express connected operations. Avoid delivery cues, food photography as the primary identity, fabricated UI, and unrelated warm colors.

The canvas geometry, safe placements, export sizes, and copy live in ../source/generate_assets.py and each editable SVG master.
