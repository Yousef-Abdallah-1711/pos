# SERVIO financial recovery constitution

## Evidence and traceability

Preserve original financial issue IDs and source text. Link every remediation or acceptance claim to the exact code revision, scenario, and evidence. Record unknowns explicitly; a commit or passing CI alone does not establish that the original operating discrepancy is resolved.

## Financial integrity and accounting authority

Financial transactions must be tenant scoped, auditable, balanced, and tested against independently derived reconciliation assertions. OPEN-01, EARN-01, and CLOSE-01 remain pending until authorized owners record approval. Do not activate policy-dependent production behavior without approval. CFR-010 remains open and financial production NO-GO.

## Safe execution

Use disposable data and environments for reproduction and tests. Do not alter production financial records, publish credentials or sensitive evidence, or discard local work. Preserve relevant changes separately and verify remote locations. Follow repository coding standards and branch protection.

## Delivery and review

Specifications, plans, and ordered tasks must include normal, edge, failure, concurrency, authorization, and reconciliation scenarios where applicable. Record missing evidence and approvals as blockers. Require independent review when requested; do not self-approve or bypass branch protection.

## Governance

This constitution governs the recovery package; CODING_STANDARDS.md and repository instructions remain applicable. Changes require a reviewed PR. This text does not constitute accounting approval or production acceptance.
